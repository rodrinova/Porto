The 23rd

For PIERRE & MARYIA

A small Porto, put together by Inês & Rodrigo for the week you crossed Europe for us.

Dear Pierre,
dear Maryia,

Let's get the facts out of the way. You took time off work, bought flights, packed a suit that will spend most of the week folded, and crossed half of Europe to watch a man who dances like a filing cabinet promise to love someone in public. People have been given medals for less.

Pierre, about Switzerland. I arrived having never really lived on my own, and left with a rough idea of who I was. You were there for most of the experiment. You also brought the bottles, so that we could "learn about wine". We learned. Some nights we learned twice.

We talk rarely, which in our case is a feature: every time we do, it feels like picking up a sentence from the day before. That is a rare thing, and I don't intend to lose it to something as weak as distance.

Maryia, thank you for opening your home to us last year, for the patience, and for being the reason Pierre now has better taste in everything except jokes.

Last summer you showed us your country like hosts who had rehearsed. We can't promise the trains here run to the minute. We can promise the wine is cheaper and the churches are older. One of us will be busy getting married and the other will be busy making sure he turns up, so this little guide will have to do some of the hosting for us. It knows where you are, what time it is, and when the light gets good. It also knows where Rodrigo has left fingerprints on the city. Look up when it tells you to.

Use it, ignore it, get lost on purpose. Porto rewards that.

We are very, very glad you're coming.

Com um abraço enorme,
Inês & RodrigoPorto · October 2026
